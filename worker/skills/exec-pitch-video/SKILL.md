# Content before pictures

Adapted from the user-supplied exec-pitch-video workflow and VIDEO_SKILLS_REGISTRY_20261013.
The attachments document another environment; its bot scheduler, paths and publishing authority do not apply here.

1. Preserve the complete brief. Establish audience, purpose, one thesis, a causal chain, and evidence boundaries.
2. Write developed Chinese arguments and a concise natural spoken script before planning shots. English instructions, Chinese deliverables.
3. Cite literal source excerpts. No invented numbers, measured ROI, customer cases, screenshots, or unapproved paid media.
4. Keep factual exclusions in the spoken script. Identify inference and proposed actions as such.
5. Derive the storyboard from the approved script; preserve narration, section order, duration and quotes verbatim.
6. Use meaningful visible state changes, varied shot layouts and native portrait composition. A palette is not a repeated layout.
7. Optional Microsoft voice is synthesized before storyboard timing; use measured natural duration and reuse cached audio in the final mix. Never silently replace a failed voice with silence or another engine. Do not truncate or accelerate online speech.
8. Package the content manuscript, narration, subtitles, storyboard, contact sheet and real MP4. Decode and inspect output before settlement.
9. Automated integrity checks do not establish factual or aesthetic approval. Human review remains explicit.
10. Claude content mode supports 6–180 seconds. Live-action mode must select reviewed footage IDs, cut on actions, use at least 60 percent actual moving footage and never loop/freeze/pad to duration. No paid/generative assets without approval.
11. Keep a source-supported question, action, reversal/decision and bounded ending. Stock is illustrative, never a real customer event or product endorsement. Use source-scene-video for visual selection; retain all provenance and framing checks.
